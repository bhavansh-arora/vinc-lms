Why now

Skill education is now part of every CBSE timetable

CBSE has asked every affiliated school to run skill education in Grades 6–8 with NCERT's Kaushal Bodh books. That means 110 hours a year, three hands-on projects, and assessment built on projects rather than exams. Most schools have the books. Few have a way to run, track and grade it all. VINC Lab is that way.

    FAQ

    Questions schools ask

    Does it follow the Kaushal Bodh book?

    Yes. Every activity points to its textbook page.

    Can parents see how their child is doing?

    Yes: scores, photos and teacher feedback.

    Still curious? Ask us or write to info@vinc-lab.com.

    Is VINC Lab part of CBSE or NCERT?

    No. VINC Lab is an independent platform built around NCERT's Kaushal Bodh activity books and the implementation guidance in CBSE circulars. Students still use the textbook. Every activity in VINC Lab points to its page.

    How is the assessment split?

    Following CBSE Circular Skill-81/2025: written test 10%, oral presentation or viva 30%, activity book 30%, portfolio 10% and teacher observation 20%. VINC Lab adds these up for every project and for the year.

    Do teachers need special training?

    No. Kaushal Bodh is taught by your existing teachers. VINC Lab gives them the plan for each class, the viva questions to ask and one-tap rubrics. VINC also runs optional teacher workshops.

    Can we use our own name and colours?

    Yes. Each school gets its own studio name, colour and badge. Students and parents see your brand; "Powered by VINC Lab" sits quietly in the footer.

    What do parents see?

    Their child's projects, photos from the portfolio, scores for each part of the assessment, teacher feedback and ideas to try at home.

    Which grades are covered?

    Grades 6, 7 and 8: all 18 Kaushal Bodh projects, from the School Kitchen Garden and Scratch games in Grade 6, to the AI Assistant in Grade 7 and Arduino Home Automation in Grade 8. Each comes with activities, a quiz, viva prompts and rubrics.

    What does a teacher get?

    A portal with only their own sections, organised grade by grade: what to teach next and on which page, a session-by-session lesson plan, which quiz questions the class finds hard, how the class is doing on the work values, who needs a nudge, and one-tap marking.

    Where is the data stored in this preview?

    This preview runs entirely in your browser with demo accounts, so nothing you enter leaves your device. Pilot schools get a hosted version with secure logins.
